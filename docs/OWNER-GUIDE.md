# Working with the research harness

Talk to the agent as you would to a research collaborator. You do not need to
name a mode, fill in a template, manage notes, edit ledgers, or make commits.
The agent infers scope from ordinary language and handles repository mechanics.

## Adapting the template

The included contract, sample thread, workflow documents, and environment notes
are starting points. Describe how you prefer to work, discuss changes with the
agent, or simply work normally and correct mismatches as they appear. The agent
can revise the repository to fit your terminology, policies, tools, execution
environment, and working habits. You do not need to translate those preferences
into configuration files yourself.

## Common interactions

> Catch up on the sample inquiry and tell me what you want to do.

The agent reads the relevant canonical state, proposes a substantive direction,
and waits before beginning a sustained attack.

> That seems worthwhile. Go for it.

The agent begins the proposed investigation and persists beyond the first
superficial failure.

> This route is too narrow. Discuss a pivot.

The agent preserves any durable result, identifies what failed, and proposes a
materially different mechanism.

> Record this as progress.

The agent treats the package as owner-significant and updates canonical state
when appropriate. This does not mean the owner checked its correctness.

> I checked this proof.

The exact checked claim may become `proved/owner-checked`. General praise does
not carry that meaning.

> Put this in the paper.

The agent may edit and compile the live paper. Recording progress, preparing a
research note, or writing a standalone report does not independently authorize
paper editing.

## What the records mean

- The project foundations file is the compact common mathematical boot sheet
  read before every thread. It records stable cross-thread conventions,
  results, and scope guards while leaving exact evidence in the owning claim
  catalog.
- Scratch holds disposable reasoning and probes.
- A note preserves one coherent durable package.
- A ledger describes the present research picture in one sitting.
- A claim catalog stores exact reusable statements and provenance.
- Active state organizes interacting live routes without becoming evidence.
- Git preserves the evolution between coherent checkpoints.

Correctness and significance remain separate. The owner controls durable
direction and owner-checking; the agent keeps factual state current at the
evidence level actually established.

## Four scopes of work

- A bounded task produces a requested artifact or service without creating a
  broader agenda.
- A directed investigation seriously explores one question or mechanism and
  returns with a result, precise obstruction, useful pivot, or honest
  diminishing-return judgment.
- A full-resolution campaign targets one exact theorem or conjecture. It begins
  after approval of a short target-and-scope contract. Narrow cases and bounded
  computations are progress, not completion.
- A theory-development program grants standing autonomy in an approved
  mathematical territory. It may generate useful definitions, theorems,
  conjectures, examples, and focused subcampaigns.

See [`RESEARCH-MODES.md`](RESEARCH-MODES.md) for the authority boundaries.

## Literature

Source discovery is normally part of an authorized research task. The agent
checks the local catalog first, searches suitable public sources, controls the
version used, and distinguishes finding a related statement from verifying the
exact claim needed.

A claim becomes `proved/literature` only after its statement, assumptions,
scope, and conventions match an identified source. Local possession of a source
does not imply that you knew or endorsed it. See
[`LITERATURE.md`](LITERATURE.md) for the complete workflow.

## Computation and collaboration

When computation becomes durable, the agent records its exact scope, command,
versions, conventions, machine-readable output, and human-readable
interpretation. See
[`COMPUTATIONS.md`](COMPUTATIONS.md).

A lead agent may optionally delegate independent tasks to internal subagents and
remains responsible for auditing and synthesis. The `briefings/` interface
supports collaborators without repository access. Neither mechanism enlarges
the research scope you authorized. See
[`COLLABORATION.md`](COLLABORATION.md).
