# Mathematical writing examples

Use these passages when calibrating the style of an owner-directed paper. The
owner's own writing is the primary exemplar. Other passages illustrate useful
alternatives, not uniform styles of mathematical schools or rules based on an
author's first language. Each row is a bounded observation from a complete
proof passage and its surrounding exposition, checked on a recorded date. Read
the linked passage rather than copying its stock phrases. Source files stay in
the ignored reference workspace; versioned identifiers let the literature
workflow recover them.

This file is writer-side calibration material. Do not give it to an isolated
reader: the manuscript repair examples below would disclose expected findings.
The portable reader question is sufficient for an independent review.

The sample harness ships no checked passages. Fill the tables from the owner's
papers and from papers the owner names as models.

## Applying the examples to a manuscript

Editing examples from manuscript discussions, not quotations from the sources
above and not global substitutions.

| Compressed wording | Possible revision and when it helps |
| --- | --- |
| "Adjunction makes f right almost split." | "By adjunction, f is right almost split." If the transferred factorization is not immediate for the audience, explain which maps factor through f. |
| "Hom(E,Y)=0 makes the Ext map injective." | "Since Hom(E,Y)=0, the long exact sequence shows that the indicated Ext map is injective." The short exact sequence and the Ext map must already be specified. |
| "The functor preserves projectives." | Keep it when it states the exact property used. Replacing a precise mathematical subject with "we" gains nothing. |

Read a revised paragraph as a whole: what is chosen, which operation is
performed, why it applies, and what follows? Familiar words may repeat. Do not
add steps that are already immediate, turn this into a search-and-replace rule,
or treat corpus frequency as evidence that a passage is readable.
